Player capture test page

A small player that feeds real H.264 and AAC segments (12 seconds of Big Buck Bunny, see mse/README.md) to the browser through Media Source Extensions, the way a streaming site does. Nothing here is a stream the extension can fetch on its own: the video exists only inside the page's player. Options in the address: ?switch=1 changes the picture's quality in the middle, ?eme=1 asks for encrypted media like a protected stream.
